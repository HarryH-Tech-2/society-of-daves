David in Welsh is Dafydd: David in other languages, from Dewi to Dawud

Wherever you go, there is a Dave. He just spells it differently.

By the Society of Daves. 26 October 2026. Updated 2 November 2026. 2 minute read.

The short version

  • David in Welsh is Dafydd, pronounced roughly "DAH-vith". Dewi is the form used for Saint David (Dewi Sant), and Dai is the usual short form.
  • David has a local form in almost every language: Dàibhidh (Scottish Gaelic), Davide (Italian), Dawid (Polish), Davit (Georgian, Armenian), Dawud (Arabic), Kawika (Hawaiian).
  • The name travelled so widely because King David is honoured in Judaism, Christianity and Islam.

David is one of the most international names there is. King David is honoured in Judaism, Christianity and Islam (more on that in what the name Dave means), so the name has a local form in almost every language those traditions reached. Here's how to say Dave around the world.

What is David in Welsh?

David in Welsh is Dafydd. It's pronounced roughly "DAH-vith", because Welsh spelling works differently from English: a single f is said like an English "v", and dd is the "th" sound in "this". So Dafydd is not "Daff-id", whatever your satnav says.

Welsh has two more forms you'll meet:

  • Dewi (said "DEH-wee") is an older Welsh form, and the one used for the patron saint: Saint David is Dewi Sant, and St David's Day is Dydd Gŵyl Dewi. See our guide to St David's Day.
  • Dai (said like "dye") is the everyday short form, the Welsh equivalent of Dave.

Wales takes the name seriously. Famous Welsh Dafydds include the 14th-century poet Dafydd ap Gwilym, often called the greatest poet in the Welsh language; Dafydd ap Gruffudd, the last native Prince of Wales, who died in 1283; and the folk singer Dafydd Iwan, whose "Yma o Hyd" became an anthem for Wales football fans.

David in the Caucasus

In Georgian and Armenian the name is Davit. In Georgia it carries special weight because of King David IV, "the Builder", one of the country's great national heroes.

David in Hawaiian

In Hawaiian, David becomes Kawika. Hawaiian has fewer consonants than English, so names get adapted: D becomes K, and v becomes w. It's possibly the most fun way to be a Dave.

Which version of Dave are you?

Under Article 1 of the Society's bylaws, Daves, Davids, Davys, Daveys, Dais and Dafydds are all full members. We've taken the liberty of extending that to every form on this page. Kawikas, welcome.

Frequently asked questions

What is David in Welsh?

David is Dafydd in Welsh, pronounced roughly "DAH-vith". Dewi is the form used for Saint David (Dewi Sant), and Dai is a common short form.

How do you pronounce Dafydd?

Roughly "DAH-vith". In Welsh a single f sounds like an English v, and dd sounds like the "th" in "this".

Is David a Welsh name?

David comes from Hebrew, but it has been a Welsh favourite for centuries because Saint David (Dewi Sant) is the patron saint of Wales. The Welsh forms are Dafydd, Dewi and Dai.

What is David in Arabic?

David is Dawud (also written Daoud) in Arabic, داود. In Islam, Dawud is honoured as a prophet and king.

What is David in Hawaiian?

David is Kawika in Hawaiian.

What is David in Italian?

David is Davide in Italian.
