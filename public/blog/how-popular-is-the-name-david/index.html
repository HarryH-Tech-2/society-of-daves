How many Davids are there in the world? The name David in numbers

The data behind the feeling that there was a Dave in every classroom. There was.

By the Society of Daves. 26 October 2026. Updated 2 November 2026. 3 minute read.

The short version

  • About 13.4 million people in the world are called David, according to the name-statistics site Forebears. That makes it the 13th most common first name on Earth, before you count Dafydds, Davides and Dawids.
  • The US has the most Davids, about 4.6 million, followed by England with about 1.2 million.
  • About 3.7 million boys named David were born in the US between 1880 and 2025, according to Social Security records.
  • The peak decade was the 1950s, with about 770,000 Davids. The peak year was 1955, with about 86,000.
  • David was the number one boys' name in the US in 1960. In 2024 it ranked 31st, with about 7,300 births.

About 13.4 million people in the world are called David, which makes it the 13th most common first name on the planet. In the US alone, about 3.7 million boys named David have been born since records began in 1880. The name peaked in the 1950s, briefly took the top spot in 1960, and is still in the top 40 today.

How many Davids are there in the world?

Nobody runs a global register of Daves (the Society is working on it), but the best estimate comes from Forebears, which counts first names across a database of more than four billion people. Its figure is about 13.4 million Davids worldwide, ranking David 13th among all the first names in the world.

That 13.4 million counts only the spelling David. It leaves out the Dafydds, Davides, Dawids, Davits and Dawuds (see David in other languages), so the real number of Daves in spirit is higher still.

How many Daves are there?

If you mean people whose given name is literally Dave, not David, there are far fewer: Forebears puts it at about 308,000 worldwide, mostly in the US, Canada and the Philippines. Most Daves are Davids on paper; see is Dave short for David?

How many Davids are there in the US?

Forebears counted about 4.6 million Davids in the US. A different method gives a lower figure for those still alive: HowManyOfMe takes the Social Security birth records and adjusts for life expectancy, and estimates about 2.8 million living Americans named David, or roughly 1 in 120 people. The two figures measure different things and come from different years, so treat both as estimates.

When was David most popular?

The 1950s were peak Dave. About 770,000 Davids were born in the US that decade, and the 1960s were barely behind with about 734,000. The single busiest year was 1955, with about 86,000 baby Davids.

That means if you went to school in the 1960s or 70s, there was a good chance of more than one David in your class. This is where Article 5 of the bylaws, the "Other Dave" rule, comes from.

Was David ever the number one name?

Yes. In 1960, David was the most popular name for baby boys in the United States, with 85,929 births, according to the Social Security Administration's baby name data. It narrowly beat Michael, which held the top spot for almost every year from the mid-1950s to the late 1990s. For one year, the Daves won.

How popular is David now?

David is less dominant than it was, but it's far from rare. In 2024 it ranked 31st among US boys' names, with about 7,300 births. That's roughly a twelfth of its 1955 peak, but it's still more Davids every year than most names manage in a decade. You can look up the year-by-year figures on Name Almanac.

Which means there are a lot of Davids born in the 1950s and 60s with a birthday coming up. Our gift guide for Daves can help, and so can a Society gift membership.

Why did David become less popular?

David didn't fall out of favour so much as the field got bigger. American parents now choose from a much wider range of names than they did in the 1950s, so even the top names have a smaller share of babies than they used to. David has held up better than many names from its era, and it has stayed in the top 50 for decades.

Frequently asked questions

How many people are called David in the world?

About 13.4 million people worldwide are called David, according to Forebears, making it the 13th most common first name in the world. That excludes other spellings such as Dafydd, Davide and Dawid.

How many Daves are there in the world?

About 308,000 people have Dave as their actual given name, according to Forebears. Most Daves are Davids on paper, and there are about 13.4 million Davids worldwide.

Which country has the most Davids?

The United States, with about 4.6 million people called David, followed by England with about 1.2 million (Forebears, 2014 data).

How popular is the name David?

David ranked 31st among US boys' names in 2024, with about 7,300 births. It is one of the most-used boys' names in American history, with about 3.7 million born since 1880.

When was the name David most popular?

David peaked in the 1950s, with about 770,000 US births in that decade. The single peak year was 1955, and David was the number one boys' name in 1960.

Was David ever the most popular boys' name?

Yes. David was the most popular name for baby boys in the United States in 1960, with 85,929 births, just ahead of Michael.
