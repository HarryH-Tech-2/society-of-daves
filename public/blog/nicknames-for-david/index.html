Nicknames for David: Dave, Davy, Dai and every other short form

Most Davids answer to at least three names. Here is where every one of them comes from.

By the Society of Daves. 17 November 2026. 3 minute read.

The short version

  • The most common nicknames for David are Dave, Davey, Davy and Davie. Dave is the one most people use today.
  • In Wales, David becomes Dai or Daf for short, and Dewi is the Welsh form used for Saint David.
  • Older and international short forms include Daw (medieval English), Taavi (Finnish and Estonian) and Dudel (Yiddish).
  • Informal versions include Davo, popular in Australia, and simply D.

The most common nicknames for David are Dave, Davey, Davy and Davie. Dave is by far the most used today, and Davie is the traditional Scottish one. In Wales, a David is often Dai or Daf. Go back far enough, or travel far enough, and you'll find Daw, Taavi and Dudel too. Here's every short form of David the Society could verify, and where each one comes from.

The classic English nicknames for David

  • Dave. The default nickname, and the one on most name badges. It's so common that some people are given Dave as their full name; more on that in what the name Dave means.
  • Davy. The older spelling, best known from Davy Crockett, the frontiersman born David Crockett in 1786, and from Davy Jones of the Monkees, who was born David Jones.
  • Davey. The same name as Davy with an extra e. Neither spelling is more correct.
  • Davie. The Scottish form, and the traditional nickname for David in Scotland.

Welsh nicknames for David

Wales has a long love of the name, thanks to its patron saint, so it's no surprise it has short forms of its own:

  • Dai (said "die"). The classic Welsh nickname for David or Dafydd. The Welsh hurdler Dai Greene, the 2011 world champion, was born David Greene.
  • Daf. A short form of Dafydd, the Welsh spelling of David.
  • Dewi. Strictly a Welsh form of the name rather than a nickname, and the one used for Saint David, Dewi Sant. See our guide to St David's Day.
  • Dewey. An English name that grew out of the Welsh Dewi.
  • Taffy. Comes from Dafydd, but it's now mostly a slang word for any Welsh person, and many people find it offensive. Best not used for your Welsh colleague.

Old and international short forms of David

  • Daw. A medieval English nickname for David. It lives on in surnames such as Dawson, "son of Daw".
  • Taavi. The Finnish and Estonian short form, from Taavetti in Finnish and Taavet in Estonian.
  • Dudel. A Yiddish form of David, alongside the more formal Dovid.

For the full forms in other languages, from Dafydd to Dawud to Kawika, see David in other languages.

Informal and modern nicknames for David

Every Dave collects a few of these at school or at work. Nobody keeps an official list, but you'll hear them everywhere:

  • Davo. The Australian version, made the Australian way: shorten the name, add an o.
  • D. Just the initial. Even the David in Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 goes by it.
  • Big Dave and Little Dave. The traditional solution when an office has two Daves, regardless of the Daves' actual sizes.
  • Dave plus a surname initial. Dave B, Dave S. The solution when an office has three Daves.

Surnames that started with David

Several common surnames began as "son of David" in one form or another. Davidson and Davis both come from David, and Dawson comes from the medieval nickname Daw. So if you're a Dave Davis, you're about as Dave as it's possible to be.

Davey, Davy or Davie: which spelling is right?

All three are correct, and they're the same nickname. Davy is the older, famous spelling, Davey is the same name with an extra e, and Davie is the Scottish form. If you're writing to a particular David, use the one they use. If they haven't said, Dave is the safe choice.

When is David's name day?

In several countries that celebrate name days, David's falls on 29 or 30 December. In Spain and Croatia it's 1 March, the feast of Saint David of Wales. That makes 1 March a good day to do something nice for a David, whatever they call themselves, and a Society gift membership can be scheduled to arrive that day.

What is the Welsh nickname for David?
