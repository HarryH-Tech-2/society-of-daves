Why is D called David in Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2?

A new Dave has entered Night City. The Society has questions, and one big spoiler warning.

By the Society of Daves. 2 November 2026. 2 minute read.

The short version

  • D, the nomad netrunner in Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 (2026), is also called David.
  • The writers built him as a parallel to David Martinez, the hero of the first Cyberpunk: Edgerunners. Both want revenge, and both go after Arasaka.
  • The show's creators haven't given a separate reason for the name. The echo of the first series is the point.

D, the netrunner at the centre of Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2, is also called David. If that rings a bell, it's meant to. The first series followed a different David, David Martinez, and the new show deliberately sets the two side by side. Here's what we know, from the Society's official position of having opinions about anyone called David.

Spoiler warning: this post discusses how the second series ends.

Who is D?

D is a nomad netrunner from the Snake Nation clan. After his clan is massacred, he sets out to find and punish the man responsible. The character was created by the series' writer and showrunner, Bartosz Sztybor, with director Kai Ikarashi, for the 2026 series from Studio Trigger and CD Projekt Red. He's voiced by Koki Uchiyama in Japanese and Nazeeh Tarsha in the English dub.

His fellow survivor Omi, another former member of the clan, knew him before everything changed.

Why is D named David?

As far as we can find, the creators haven't explained the name in so many words. But the reason is hard to miss: D was written as a parallel to David Martinez, the hero of the first Cyberpunk: Edgerunners. Both are young men driven by revenge, and both end up going after the Arasaka corporation, but they go about it very differently. Giving D the same first name makes the comparison impossible to ignore, and reviewers at Radio Times and GamesRadar picked up on how the two characters' final choices mirror each other.

The biggest spoiler is also the clearest link between them. D eventually learns that the man behind his clan's massacre, Tanaka, was already killed by David Martinez's crew. His revenge was taken before he got there, and that leaves him empty in the final act. Sztybor reportedly wanted D's ending to be more ambiguous and more hopeful than the first David's.

Is D the same person as David Martinez?

No. They're two different characters who share a first name. That's the source of much of the confusion online, and of most of the searches that brought you here.

What does the name David mean?

David is a Hebrew name, usually translated as "beloved". That's an interesting choice for two characters whose lives go so badly, and we'd like to think the writers knew it. The full story is in what the name Dave means, and the real-life Davids are in famous Daves.

Does D count as a Dave?

Under Article 1 of the Society's bylaws, anyone called David is a full member, and nicknames are no barrier. D is therefore welcome, though the Society would ask him to leave the cyberware at the door.
