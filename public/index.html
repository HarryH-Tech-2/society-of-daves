What does the name Dave mean?

Dave is short for David, a Hebrew name usually translated as "beloved". Here's everything else a Dave should know about being a Dave. All of it is true, which the Society finds more surprising than anyone.

  1. Where it comes from

    David comes from the Hebrew Dawid (דָּוִד), usually linked to the word dod, meaning "beloved". Dave is the short form, although the Society regards it as the definitive edition. So, technically, your mum was right.

  2. The original Dave

    King David of Israel, who in the Bible beats the giant Goliath with a sling and one stone. He went on to become a king, a musician and a poet, setting a standard most subsequent Daves have politely declined to meet. He's honoured in Judaism, Christianity and Islam, where he is the prophet Dawud, and that's why the name spread so far.

  3. The peak Dave year

    In 1960, David was the most popular name for baby boys in the United States: 85,929 of them, according to Social Security records. It narrowly beat Michael, which won almost every other year of that era. If you went to school in the 60s, you already knew this.

  4. A day of your own

    Saint David is the patron saint of Wales, and his feast day is 1 March. His most famous advice was "do the little things". It is not a public holiday. The Society is looking into it. More on St David's Day.

  5. The tallest Dave

    Michelangelo carved his statue of David in Florence between 1501 and 1504, from a single block of marble. It is 5.17 metres tall, making it the tallest Dave on record, and also the least dressed.

  6. A TV channel

    In 2007 a British TV channel renamed itself Dave, on the reasoning that everybody knows a bloke called Dave. The Society agrees with this reasoning completely. (Since 2024 it has been called U&Dave, which we're choosing not to take personally.)

  7. The nicknames

    Davy and Davie are the older short forms (see Davy Crockett, born 1786). Dai is the Welsh one. Dave is the one on most name badges today, and some people have it as their full name.

A century of Daves

About 3.7 million boys named David have been born in the US since 1880. The name peaked in the 1950s, when nearly 4 in every 100 American baby boys were Davids. In 2024 it still ranked 31st. Hover over or tap a bar for the numbers.

Notable Daves

None of these Daves are members, and none of them have endorsed this website. We remain hopeful.

Born David Jones. He changed his surname because the Monkees already had a singer called Davy Jones. The rare case of a Dave with too many Daves around him.

Has been explaining the natural world to the rest of us since presenting Zoo Quest in 1954. The most trusted voice any Dave has ever had.

Drummer in Nirvana, then founder of Foo Fighters, whose first album he recorded in about a week, playing almost every instrument himself. A one-Dave band.

Daves in films, TV and music

Popular culture has been talking to Daves for decades. Not always kindly.

  • "I'm sorry, Dave. I'm afraid I can't do that."

    HAL 9000 to astronaut Dave Bowman, 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968). Still the most famous thing ever said to a Dave.

  • "Dave's not here."

    Cheech & Chong's 1971 comedy routine, in which someone called Dave can't get into his own home.

  • Dave (1993)

    Kevin Kline plays an ordinary man asked to stand in for the US President because he looks just like him. A documentary about the hidden potential of every Dave.

  • Red Dwarf (1988)

    Dave Lister wakes up three million years in the future as the last human alive. He's still a Dave.

  • Psychodrama (2019)

    The debut album by the British rapper Dave won the Mercury Prize. He goes by just "Dave", which takes confidence.

The bylaws

Adopted unanimously at the founding meeting, which was attended by several Daves and one man who thought it was a different meeting.

  1. Membership is open to all Daves, Davids, Davys, Daveys, Dais and Dafydds, wherever they may be, and in whatever language they spell it.
  2. A David who insists on being called David is still a Dave. The Society will respect their wishes in writing only.
  3. Anyone not called Dave may join as an Honorary Dave, and will be addressed as Dave in all correspondence.
  4. People with Dave or David as a surname may apply for observer status. See Larry David.
  5. When two members work in the same office, the one who arrived first keeps the name. The other becomes Other Dave.
  6. Dues are one dollar a month. The Society's treasurer is also called Dave.
  7. Members shall do the little things, as Saint David instructed, and shall not be made to explain which Dave they are more than once per meeting.

Questions Daves ask

What does the name Dave mean?

Dave is short for David, which comes from the Hebrew name Dawid and is usually translated as "beloved". It comes from King David of Israel, who is honoured in Judaism, Christianity and Islam.

Is Dave short for David?

Yes. Dave is the most common short form of David. Older short forms include Davy, Davie and the Welsh Dai, and some people are given Dave as their full name.

How popular is the name David?

About 3.7 million boys named David have been born in the US since 1880. It was the number one boys' name in 1960, peaked in the 1950s, and ranked 31st in 2024.

When is St David's Day?

St David's Day is on 1 March. It celebrates Saint David, the patron saint of Wales. It is not a public holiday.

Is the Society of Daves a real society?

The membership, the newsletter and the payments are real. The pomp is not. This is a parody site run for fun, and it also tells you true things about your name.
